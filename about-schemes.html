Academy Membership

How it works

You earn colours by playing rounds. Three things, and that's really all of it.

  1. Play your roundLog your score in the app, hole by hole.
  2. Beat the targetEach colour has a score to beat. Come in at or under it and the colour is yours — it goes in your trophy cabinet and stays there.
  3. One colour at a timeYou earn colours in order. A brilliant round doesn't skip you ahead — it earns you the next one waiting.

Golf Gateway

Where everyone starts. Three stages, three colours each, and the course gets longer as you climb.

Targets are measured in strokes over par — par being the score a very good golfer would take. If par for three holes is 12 and you go round in 15, you're three over.

Where you start from. Par 3s from the red tee. Par 4s from the 150 yard marker. Par 5s from the 200 yard marker.

Golf Gateway Plus

Nine holes off the red tees — every hole — and the same nine colours again, this time with a plus.

No separate stages here. It's one long stretch, and the targets tighten by two strokes each time: 30 over par for Maroon+, all the way down to 14 for Platinum+.

Questions we get asked

What if I have a bad round?

Nothing happens. You keep every colour you've earned. Play again whenever you like.

Can I lose a colour?

Only if a score was written down wrong and your Club Pro corrects it. Otherwise a colour is yours for good.

Why didn't I get two colours at once?

Your club awards one colour per round — the next one waiting for you. Even a very low score earns just that one, so there's always something to aim at next time.

Who can see my scores?

You and your Club Pro, always. Players in your group can see each other's rounds. If you're under 18 and a parent or carer has been linked to your account by the club, they can see your rounds and awards too.
