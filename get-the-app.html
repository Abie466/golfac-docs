Academy Membership

Get the app on your phone

It runs in your web browser. There’s nothing to download from an app store.

1Open the app

Open ga.golfinstitute.uk

Reading this on a computer? Scan the code with your phone’s camera instead.

The address is always ga.golfinstitute.uk

Sign in with the email address your invitation was sent to, and your password.

2Put it on your home screen

Do this once. Choose your phone:

iPhone use Safari

  1. Open ga.golfinstitute.uk in Safari.
  2. Tap Share. Can’t see it? Tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.

Android use Chrome

  1. Open ga.golfinstitute.uk in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to home screen or Install app, then confirm.

From now on, open it from its icon, like any other app.

Forgotten your password?

On the sign-in page, tap Forgot password, enter your email address and follow the link we email you. Not arrived after a few minutes? Check your junk folder.

Lost the web address?

It’s always ga.golfinstitute.uk. Your Club Pro can send you this guide again.

Still stuck? Ask your Club Pro.
